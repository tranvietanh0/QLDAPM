# 1. Project Overview

## Ten du an
Intern Management System (IMS) - He thong quan ly thuc tap sinh.

## Muc tieu
So hoa quy trinh quan ly thuc tap sinh tu luc tiep nhan, phan mentor, giao task, bao cao hang tuan, danh gia den khi ket thuc ky thuc tap.

## Pham vi MVP
- Dang nhap va phan quyen ADMIN/Mentor/Intern.
- Quan ly danh sach thuc tap sinh.
- Phan Mentor va Department cho Intern.
- Mentor tao Task.
- Intern cap nhat trang thai Task.
- Intern nop Weekly Report.
- Mentor review bao cao.
- Mentor danh gia cuoi ky.
- Dashboard thong ke.

## Ngoai pham vi
- Cham cong GPS/biometric.
- Payroll/luong.
- Tuyen dung ATS day du.
- Email/SMS that.
- AI tu dong cham diem.

## Stakeholders
- Admin/HR.
- Mentor.
- Intern.
- Giang vien/nguoi cham do an.

## Thanh cong du an
- Tat ca luong demo chinh chay end-to-end.
- Khong cho phep role truy cap sai nghiep vu.
- Business rule task deadline nam trong ky thuc tap.
- Test case quan trong dat PASS truoc demo.
